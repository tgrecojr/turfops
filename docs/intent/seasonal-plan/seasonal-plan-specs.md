# Seasonal Plan & Calendar — EARS Specs

Design: [seasonal-plan-design.md](seasonal-plan-design.md). Prefix `PLAN`. Facets: `CROSS` (10 cm crossing detection and aggregation), `CACHE` (crossing cache and its fill), `ACT` (the plan's own 10 cm activities: windows, completion, status, details), `MERGE` (`GET /api/v1/seasonal-plan` assembly), `CAL` (`GET /api/v1/applications/calendar`), `UI` (Seasonal Plan page), `CALUI` (Calendar page).

"Daily 10 cm means" are the weather lake's gold daily 10 cm soil means (Weather data access contract, WX-SRC-LAKE-007). A threshold's "median" is its aggregated median crossing date mapped onto the plan year (PLAN-CROSS-006). The "plan year" is the `year` the seasonal plan is built for; the "current season" is the plan year equal to the server's local current year. A "complete year" is a year the crossing cache holds as settled (PLAN-CACHE-005). A "turf application" is one for which `Application::is_turf()` holds (no `plant_id` and not a plant-only type). "10 cm activities" are the activities this component computes itself; "timing activities" are those the Timing windows contribute (TIME-PLAN-001…007), which this file does not restate.

## 10 cm crossing detection

- [x] **PLAN-CROSS-001**: The 10 cm crossing analysis shall read one calendar year of daily 10 cm means and shall not analyze a year with fewer than 30 such days.
- [x] **PLAN-CROSS-002**: The 10 cm crossing analysis shall compute a trailing mean over each 7 consecutive daily rows, stamped on the seventh row's date, and shall find no crossings in a year with fewer than 7 rows.
- [ ] **PLAN-CROSS-003**: The 10 cm crossing analysis shall detect the rising thresholds `soil_50f_rising`, `soil_55f_rising`, `soil_60f_rising`, `soil_65f_rising` and `soil_75f_rising` (50, 55, 60, 65 and 75°F) at the first held run (PLAN-CROSS-008) of trailing means at or above the threshold that does not begin the year's data, searching only runs whose first day is in January through June.
- [ ] **PLAN-CROSS-004**: The 10 cm crossing analysis shall detect the falling thresholds `soil_65f_falling`, `soil_55f_falling`, `soil_50f_falling` and `soil_45f_falling` (65, 55, 50 and 45°F) at the first held run (PLAN-CROSS-008) of trailing means at or below the threshold that does not begin the year's data, searching only runs whose first day is in July through December.
- [ ] **PLAN-CROSS-005**: The 10 cm crossing analysis shall record at most one crossing per threshold per year, dated on the first day of its held run and carrying the trailing mean on that day.
- [ ] **PLAN-CROSS-006**: When aggregating a threshold's crossings for a plan year, the system shall map each voting crossing (PLAN-CROSS-010) onto the plan year by its month and day (February 29 onto February 28 in a non-leap plan year), sort them, and return the median (the element at index n/2), the earliest, the latest and the sample count, or nothing when the threshold has no voting crossings.
- [x] **PLAN-CROSS-007**: The system shall rate a 10 cm activity's window confidence from the sample count of its opening threshold: 5 or more High, 3–4 Medium, fewer Low.
- [ ] **PLAN-CROSS-008**: The 10 cm crossing analysis shall count a run of trailing means past a threshold as a crossing only once it holds for 5 consecutive calendar days; a trailing mean back across the threshold, a missing date, or the end of the year's data before the fifth day ends the run without a crossing, and the search continues with the next run.
- [ ] **PLAN-CROSS-009**: If the first day of a held 10 cm run immediately follows a missing date in the year's daily 10 cm means, then the 10 cm crossing analysis shall drop that crossing, leaving the threshold with no crossing for that year, as the Timing windows drop `after_gap` crossings.
- [ ] **PLAN-CROSS-010**: When aggregating 10 cm crossings for a plan year, the system shall let only the at most 10 most recent complete years before the plan year that have cached crossings vote, so the plan year itself and any partial year never contribute, and the plan's `data_years_used` shall be the number of those voting years.

## Crossing cache

- [x] **PLAN-CACHE-001**: The crossing cache shall store one row per year and threshold name with the crossing date, the trailing mean on that date, the time it was computed and the USCRN station it was computed for.
- [x] **PLAN-CACHE-002**: When `GET /api/v1/seasonal-plan` is called with the weather lake configured, the system shall first delete every cached crossing whose station is not the configured station, including rows with no station.
- [x] **PLAN-CACHE-003**: When `GET /api/v1/seasonal-plan` is called with the weather lake configured, the system shall recompute and replace the cached crossings of every year from ten years before the server-local current year through the current year that is not settled for the configured station.
- [x] **PLAN-CACHE-004**: When replacing a year's cached crossings, the system shall delete the year's rows and insert the new ones in one transaction, so a threshold that no longer crosses disappears from the cache.
- [ ] **PLAN-CACHE-005**: The system shall treat a cached year as settled for the configured station only when every one of its rows (or its settled marker, PLAN-CACHE-008) was computed on or after January 1 of the following year by the server's local date (`chrono::Local`, governed by `TZ`), never by a UTC or database-session midnight, and at a time when the weather lake had reported a day dated December 31 of that year or later.
- [x] **PLAN-CACHE-006**: If reading a year's daily 10 cm means from the weather lake fails, or writing its crossings to the cache fails, then the system shall log a warning, keep that year's cached rows, continue with the other years, and still return the seasonal plan.
- [x] **PLAN-CACHE-007**: While the weather lake is not configured, the system shall skip the crossing cache fill and build the seasonal plan from the cached crossings as they are.
- [ ] **PLAN-CACHE-008**: When the crossing cache fill computes a year that yields no crossing rows (no threshold crossed, or fewer than 30 daily 10 cm means), the system shall record a settled marker for that year and the configured station, so a year meeting PLAN-CACHE-005 is not read from the weather lake again on every load.

## 10 cm activities

- [x] **PLAN-ACT-001**: The 10 cm activity builder shall omit an activity whose opening threshold has no cached crossing.
- [x] **PLAN-ACT-002**: The 10 cm `pre_emergent` activity shall open on the `soil_50f_rising` median and close on the `soil_60f_rising` median, or 30 days after opening when that threshold has no crossing.
- [x] **PLAN-ACT-003**: The 10 cm `spring_nitrogen` activity shall open on the `soil_55f_rising` median and close on the `soil_65f_rising` median, or 21 days after opening when that threshold has no crossing.
- [ ] **PLAN-ACT-004**: The 10 cm `spring_nitrogen` activity shall be Completed only by a feeding as defined by Applications (`Application::is_feeding()`: a turf Fertilizer application with nitrogen unrecorded or above 0) dated January through May of the plan year, so a plant fertilizer or a potash-only product does not complete it.
- [ ] **PLAN-ACT-005**: The 10 cm `spring_herbicide` activity shall open 7 days before the `soil_50f_rising` median and close 7 days after the `soil_55f_rising` median, or 28 days after opening when that threshold has no crossing, and shall be Completed by any turf PostEmergent application dated January through May of the plan year.
- [ ] **PLAN-ACT-006**: The 10 cm `grub_preventative` activity shall open on the later of the `soil_60f_rising` median and May 15, close on the `soil_75f_rising` median or July 4 when that threshold has no crossing, and be Completed by any turf GrubControl or Insecticide application in the plan year.
- [ ] **PLAN-ACT-007**: The 10 cm `core_aeration` activity shall open on the `soil_65f_falling` median, close on the `soil_50f_falling` median or 30 days after opening, and be Completed by any turf Aeration application in the plan year.
- [ ] **PLAN-ACT-008**: The 10 cm `fall_overseeding` activity shall open on the `soil_65f_falling` median, close on the `soil_50f_falling` median or 28 days after opening, and be Completed by any turf Overseed application in the plan year.
- [x] **PLAN-ACT-009**: The 10 cm fall feeding activities shall be dated from the `soil_65f_falling` median: `early_fall_fert` from the median for 21 days and `mid_fall_fert` from 21 days after the median for 21 days; `winterizer` shall run 14 days from the `soil_45f_falling` median.
- [ ] **PLAN-ACT-010**: The 10 cm `early_fall_fert`, `mid_fall_fert` and `winterizer` activities shall be Completed only by a feeding as defined by Applications (`Application::is_feeding()`) dated in September, in October, and in November through December of the plan year respectively.
- [ ] **PLAN-ACT-011**: The 10 cm `fall_herbicide` activity shall open on the `soil_65f_falling` median, close on the `soil_55f_falling` median or 28 days after opening, and be Completed by any turf PostEmergent application dated September through December of the plan year.
- [ ] **PLAN-ACT-012**: The 10 cm activity builder shall set the historical range of every 10 cm window, `spring_nitrogen` included (`soil_55f_rising` earliest to `soil_65f_rising` latest), from the opening threshold's earliest crossing to the closing threshold's latest crossing, each shifted by the window's own offsets (`spring_herbicide` −7 / +7 days); for `early_fall_fert`, `mid_fall_fert` and `winterizer`, which have no closing threshold, the range shall end at the opening threshold's latest crossing plus the window's offset; any other window whose closing threshold has no crossing shall have no latest bound.
- [x] **PLAN-ACT-013**: Except where PLAN-ACT-021 takes a current-season status from an agronomic rule, the 10 cm activity builder shall set each activity's status to Completed when its completing application is logged, else Missed when today is after the window's close, else Active when today is on or after its open, else Upcoming, where today is the server's local date (`chrono::Local`, governed by `TZ`).
- [x] **PLAN-ACT-014**: The 10 cm activity builder shall set `ActivityDetails.rate` to an agronomic amount on feedings (`spring_nitrogen` 0.5, `early_fall_fert` 1.0, `mid_fall_fert` 0.75 and `winterizer` 1.0 lb N/1000 sqft) and on `fall_overseeding` (4 lb seed per 1,000 sq ft), to the words "Label rate" with no number on `pre_emergent`, `spring_herbicide`, `grub_preventative` and `fall_herbicide`, and to nothing on `core_aeration`.
- [x] **PLAN-ACT-015**: The 10 cm activities' descriptions, product suggestions and notes shall never state a product label rate or any pesticide rate.
- [x] **PLAN-ACT-016**: The 10 cm activity builder shall give each activity a soil-temperature trigger naming its 10 cm range as a "7-day avg at 10cm".
- [ ] **PLAN-ACT-017**: When placing the 10 cm `spring_herbicide` window edges in the plan year, the 10 cm activity builder shall change only an edge's year, keeping its month and day (February 29 becomes February 28 in a non-leap plan year), so an edge on the 29th–31st keeps its day.
- [ ] **PLAN-ACT-018**: The 10 cm activity builder shall omit `fall_herbicide` only when it is not Completed and a turf Overseed application is dated within the 60 days before its window opens (on or after 60 days before the `soil_65f_falling` median and before that median); an Overseed dated on or after the median shall not hide it.
- [ ] **PLAN-ACT-019**: The fallback 10 cm `pre_emergent` activity (on the plan only when the Timing windows return no activities, PLAN-MERGE-005) shall be Completed only by a turf PreEmergent application dated January through June of the plan year, so a fall pre-emergent does not complete it.
- [ ] **PLAN-ACT-020**: The 10 cm activity builder shall judge every 10 cm activity's completion on turf applications only (`Application::is_turf()`), so a plant Insecticide does not complete `grub_preventative` and a plant fertilizer does not complete a feeding.
- [ ] **PLAN-ACT-021**: When building the current season's plan, the 10 cm activity builder shall set the status of each activity an agronomic rule also covers — `spring_nitrogen` (spring nitrogen rule), `early_fall_fert`, `mid_fall_fert` and `winterizer` (fall fertilization rule), `spring_herbicide` and `fall_herbicide` (broadleaf herbicide rule), `grub_preventative` (grub control rule) — from the state that rule exposes for it: work logged → Completed; work due now (the rule advises doing it) → Active; window passed → Missed; otherwise Upcoming, so the plan never shows Active while the rule is silent, or the reverse.
- [ ] **PLAN-ACT-022**: When the agronomic rule covering a plan activity cannot evaluate for the current season (a required input is missing), the plan shall show the activity's typical-date status marked as an estimate.

## Seasonal plan endpoint

- [x] **PLAN-MERGE-001**: When `GET /api/v1/seasonal-plan` is called, the system shall build the plan for the `year` query parameter, defaulting to the server-local current year, and return `year`, `activities`, `data_years_used` and `generated_at`, each activity carrying `id`, `name`, `category`, `description`, a date window (`predicted_start`, `predicted_end`, optional `earliest_historical` / `latest_historical`, `confidence`), `status` and details (`soil_temp_trigger`, `product_suggestions`, `rate`, `notes`).
- [x] **PLAN-MERGE-002**: If no lawn profile exists, then `GET /api/v1/seasonal-plan` shall return 404; if the requested `year` has no valid January 1, it shall return 400.
- [x] **PLAN-MERGE-003**: When building the seasonal plan's 10 cm activities, the system shall judge completion against the default profile's applications dated from January 1 of the plan year up to January 1 of the next.
- [x] **PLAN-MERGE-004**: When the Timing windows return plan activities (TIME-PLAN-001), `GET /api/v1/seasonal-plan` shall remove every 10 cm activity whose id is in the timing `owned_ids` for the lawn's grass type (TIME-PLAN-004) and append the timing activities, so a window the log has blocked leaves neither version on the plan.
- [x] **PLAN-MERGE-005**: When the Timing windows return no plan activities (lake unconfigured, climate record unavailable, or nothing dated; TIME-PLAN-006), `GET /api/v1/seasonal-plan` shall keep all of its 10 cm activities, including `pre_emergent`, `core_aeration` and `fall_overseeding`, and shall not fail the request.
- [x] **PLAN-MERGE-006**: When building the seasonal plan, the system shall append the Landscape plants component's plant activities unchanged, passing the profile's plants, the applications from October 1 of the year before the plan year through the end of the plan year, and the server's local date as today.
- [x] **PLAN-MERGE-007**: As the last step of building the seasonal plan, the system shall sort all activities by `predicted_start` with a stable sort.
- [ ] **PLAN-MERGE-008**: When `GET /api/v1/seasonal-plan` is called, the system shall also return `today`, the server's local date (`chrono::Local`, governed by `TZ`), so the pages count down from the same date the statuses were judged on.

## Calendar endpoint

- [x] **PLAN-CAL-001**: When `GET /api/v1/applications/calendar` is called, the system shall use the `year` and `month` query parameters, each defaulting to the server's local today, and return `{year, month, days}` where `days` maps `YYYY-MM-DD` to applications.
- [x] **PLAN-CAL-002**: When `GET /api/v1/applications/calendar` is called, the system shall include every application of the default profile whose application date or follow-up date falls in the month, keyed under its application date.
- [x] **PLAN-CAL-003**: If no lawn profile exists, then `GET /api/v1/applications/calendar` shall return 404; if the year and month name no valid date, it shall return 400.

## Seasonal Plan page

- [ ] **PLAN-UI-001**: The Seasonal Plan page shall start on the server's current year (the `year` the plan endpoint returns when called without one, never the browser's clock), offer year buttons for the year before, that year and the year after, fetch the plan for the selected year, and ignore the result of a request superseded by a newer one.
- [x] **PLAN-UI-002**: While the plan loads, the Seasonal Plan page shall show "Analyzing historical soil data to build your seasonal plan..."
- [x] **PLAN-UI-003**: The Seasonal Plan page shall show the subtitle "Predicted activity windows based on N years of soil temperature data", where N is the plan's `data_years_used`.
- [x] **PLAN-UI-004**: The Seasonal Plan page shall show a summary chip for each of Active, Upcoming, Completed and Missed, in that order, that has at least one shown activity, each with its symbol (● ○ ✓ ✗), count and label.
- [x] **PLAN-UI-005**: Where any plan activity has category "Plant Maintenance" (the landscape-plant marker), the Seasonal Plan page shall offer an All / Turf / Plants filter, and the status chips and activity list shall reflect the filter.
- [x] **PLAN-UI-006**: The Seasonal Plan page shall list activities in the order the plan returns them, each card showing its status symbol and label, category, date range, name, description, a countdown, its confidence as dots and label, and a Details toggle.
- [ ] **PLAN-UI-007**: The Seasonal Plan page's countdown, counted from the plan's `today` (the server's local date, never the browser's clock), shall read "Starts tomorrow" or "Starts in N days" for Upcoming, "N days remaining" or "Window closing today" for Active, "Window has passed" for Missed, and "Done" for Completed.
- [x] **PLAN-UI-008**: When the owner expands an activity's Details on the Seasonal Plan page, the page shall show its Soil Temp Trigger, Rate, Products, Notes and Historical range, each only when present.
- [x] **PLAN-UI-009**: When no activities are shown, the Seasonal Plan page shall say "No activities could be projected for <year>. Insufficient historical data may be the cause." if the plan is empty, otherwise "No <filter> activities for <year>."
- [ ] **PLAN-UI-010**: If loading the plan fails, then the Seasonal Plan page shall show a plain-language message saying what went wrong and what to do (a missing lawn profile points to Settings; a timeout says to try again), never a raw status code or JSON body.

## Calendar page

- [ ] **PLAN-CALUI-001**: The Calendar page shall show a month grid starting on the server's current month (the `year` and `month` the calendar endpoint returns when called without them, never the browser's clock), with previous and next buttons that move across year boundaries and clear the selected day.
- [x] **PLAN-CALUI-002**: The Calendar page shall fetch the calendar endpoint for the shown month and the seasonal plan for the shown year; if the plan fetch fails, it shall render the applications without activity bars and show no error for it.
- [x] **PLAN-CALUI-003**: The Calendar page shall mark each day with a filled dot per application logged that day and a ring dot per follow-up due that day, both colored by application type and titled with the type label ("Follow-up: <type>" for rings).
- [x] **PLAN-CALUI-004**: The Calendar page shall draw on each day one bar per plan activity whose predicted window contains the day, colored by the activity's status, drawing plant activities (category "Plant Maintenance") as thin outlined bars, and shall tint the background of any day inside an activity window.
- [ ] **PLAN-CALUI-005**: The Calendar page shall never convey an activity's status by color alone: each day's activity bar shall carry the activity name with its status symbol and label in its title and accessible name, and the detail panel's status badges shall show the status symbol with the label.
- [x] **PLAN-CALUI-006**: The Calendar page shall show a legend for Applications, Follow-up due, and the Active, Upcoming, Completed and Missed bar colors with their labels.
- [x] **PLAN-CALUI-007**: When the owner selects a day that has content (by click, Enter or Space), the Calendar page shall show a detail panel with Applications (type, product, scheduled follow-up, notes), Follow-ups Due ("Originally applied <date>"), the turf activities (status, name, window, confidence, trigger, products, notes; headed "Turf Activities" when plant activities are also present, else "Planned Activities"), and Plant Maintenance (status, name, window, description, notes).
- [ ] **PLAN-CALUI-008**: If loading the calendar fails, then the Calendar page shall show a plain-language message saying what went wrong and what to do (a missing lawn profile points to Settings; a timeout says to try again), never a raw status code or JSON body.
